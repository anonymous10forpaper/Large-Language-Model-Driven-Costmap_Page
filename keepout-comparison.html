Comparison overview

We compare our custom KeepoutLayer with Nav2’s native KeepoutFilter on the same navigation task in a Gazebo factory environment. Both methods use the same start, goal, and restricted zone, with five trials per method.

Results at a glance

Both methods complete all five trials. Travel distance and time are reported as mean ± standard deviation (n = 5).
